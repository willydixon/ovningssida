How AI is used

AI helps write drafts of the scenes. A human will read and approve each scene before the site opens to everyone. Each scene shows whether it is a draft or approved, and by whom.

AI does not judge you. The tech check is ordinary measurement of sound level and light on your phone. It interprets neither your face nor your feelings.

Your acting style is also ordinary measurement on your phone, not AI: the tempo, pauses, pitch and volume of your voice. It interprets neither your face nor your feelings, and the styles are made up.

The scene partner’s voice is your phone’s built-in text-to-speech voice, the same one your phone uses to read text aloud. It runs on your phone, and the site does not send the lines anywhere. To know when you have said your line, the site only measures how loud the sound is. It does not listen to what you say.

The AI scene partner (test)

If you choose AI as your scene partner, an AI from the company ElevenLabs plays the other role and answers what you say. It has a computer-generated voice. It does not judge you and does not break the scene to give advice.

During the scene, the sound from the microphone is sent to ElevenLabs so that the AI can hear you and answer. While the AI itself is talking, silence is sent. The video is never sent.

The AI scene partner is a test. Only the person building the site can use it until the terms and privacy have been reviewed.

No video is used to train AI, since the video never leaves your phone.
