About

ActWith AI is a test version. It is for adults who want to practise scenes and record themselves. You choose a short scene, check sound and light and do a take with your phone camera. The basics are free, and Plus is available as an add-on.

What the site does not do

  • It does not judge your acting, your looks or your feelings.
  • It gives no grades and ranks no one.
  • It does not hand out roles and has no connection to castings.
  • It does not save your video. The video stays on your phone.

The site is under construction. The scenes are drafts that a knowledgeable person will review before the site opens to everyone.
