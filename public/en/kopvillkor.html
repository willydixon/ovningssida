What Plus includes

  • 60 minutes with the AI scene partner every calendar month. Minutes that are not used are not carried over to the next month.
  • A more lifelike AI voice, and conversations of up to 10 minutes at a time.
  • Takes of up to 10 minutes.
  • An unlimited number of takes in My takes, as far as there is room on your phone. The takes are only saved in the browser on your phone, never with us.

Everything that is free stays free for everyone, with or without Plus.

Price and payment

  • Plus costs 99 SEK a month, including VAT.
  • You pay in advance for one month at a time, first when you buy and then on the same day every month until you cancel Plus.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card number.
  • You must be 18 or older to buy Plus.
  • After the purchase you get a restore code. Save it: with it you get Plus back if you change phones or clear your browser.

Cancelling

There is no commitment. You cancel Plus whenever you like with the button Change card or cancel on the Plus page. You then have Plus until the end of the month you have already paid for, and after that nothing more is charged.

Right of withdrawal

  • You have a 14-day right of withdrawal from the day you buy Plus.
  • You withdraw with the button Withdraw from purchase, which is at the bottom of every page and on the Plus page. You write your name, see which purchase you are withdrawing from and confirm.
  • Within 14 days, Plus ends immediately and you get the full amount back to the card you paid with, even if you have already used Plus.
  • After 14 days, Plus is cancelled instead at the end of the month you have paid for.
  • When you have withdrawn, you immediately see a confirmation with the date and time.

If something does not work

Get in touch via Contact if Plus does not work as described here. We will then fix the fault, and if that is not possible you have the right to a price reduction or to end the purchase. You always have the rights that consumer law gives you.

If we cannot agree, you can turn to the Swedish National Board for Consumer Disputes, ARN, at arn.se.

Changes

If the price or the terms change, you will be told at least 30 days in advance. You can cancel Plus before the change takes effect.

Personal data

How we and Stripe handle your data is described on the Privacy page.

Swedish law applies to these terms.
