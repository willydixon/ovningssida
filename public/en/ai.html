How AI is used

AI helps write drafts of the scenes. A human will read and approve each scene before the site opens to everyone. Each scene shows whether it is a draft or approved, and by whom.

The tech check is ordinary measurement of sound level and light on your phone. It interprets neither your face nor your feelings.

Your acting style is also ordinary measurement on your phone, not AI: the tempo, pauses, pitch and volume of your voice. It interprets neither your face nor your feelings, and the styles are made up.

The scene partner’s voice is your phone’s built-in text-to-speech voice, the same one your phone uses to read text aloud. It runs on your phone, and the site does not send the lines anywhere. To know when you have said your line, the site only measures how loud the sound is. It does not listen to what you say.

The rating and the result

After each scene you get a rating from 1 to 10 for the role you played. It is calculated on your phone. The site measures your voice, that is tempo, pauses, pitch, volume and energy, and compares it with what the role needs. Each role has a requirement that we have set, for example a calm, low voice for a villain or lots of energy for a hero. If you have said yes to the face analysis, how your face acts counts as well.

When all five scenes in a level are rated, you get a result with a summary. The summary is written on your phone by fixed rules. It points out your strongest and weakest role, how much you changed your voice between the roles and what kept coming up, and says whether bigger or smaller roles suit you right now.

The rating is a rough guide based on measurements. It is not a human judgement of your talent, and nothing in it says whether you will succeed as an actor. No human and no language model looks at your takes. The ratings are only saved on your phone, and you can clear them whenever you like.

How your face acts

If you say yes, your phone looks at how your face acts during the take. This is done with MediaPipe, an AI model from Google that runs entirely on your phone. The files are downloaded from our own site. The model reads expressions, for example how much your eyebrows move and whether you smile, and whether your eyes are down in the script.

No images are saved or sent. After the take only two numbers are left: how much your face acted and how often your eyes were down in the script. The face analysis does not judge how you look and does not guess what you feel. If your face is not visible enough, for example in weak light, it does not count at all, neither up nor down. You can turn it off whenever you like on the recording screen.

The AI scene partner (test)

If you choose AI as your scene partner, an AI from the company ElevenLabs plays the other role and answers what you say. It has a computer-generated voice. It does not judge you and does not break the scene to give advice.

During the scene, the sound from the microphone is sent to ElevenLabs so that the AI can hear you and answer. While the AI itself is talking, silence is sent. The video is never sent.

The AI scene partner is a test. Only the person building the site can use it until the terms and privacy have been reviewed.

No video is used to train AI, since the video never leaves your phone.
