Privacy

In short: your video stays on your phone.

  • The camera and microphone are only used when you choose a role, do the tech check or record.
  • If your phone reads your partner’s lines, the site measures the sound level during the recording to know when you have said your line. The sound level is not saved, and the reading is done by the phone itself.
  • The recording stays in the browser’s memory until you save it or close the page. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • If you choose AI as your scene partner, the sound from the microphone is sent to ElevenLabs during the scene, so that the AI can hear you and answer. We have set ElevenLabs not to save the sound, but a text version of the conversation may be saved by them. The AI scene partner is a test that only the person building the site can use so far.
  • If you say yes to the face analysis, your phone reads your expressions in the camera image during the take. This is done on your phone. No images are saved or sent.
  • The site uses no cookies, no analytics service and no scripts from others.
  • Your answer about your age is not saved.
  • The web host Netlify, Inc. in the USA receives, like all web hosts, your IP address when the page loads. Netlify processes it on our behalf under its data processing agreement and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

What is saved on your phone

  • Your acting style is calculated on your phone during the take, from your voice: tempo, pauses, pitch and volume. Your looks never count. The measurements are not sent anywhere and are only saved if you save the take in My takes.
  • Takes that you save in My takes are kept in the browser’s database on your phone. They are never sent to us, and you can delete them whenever you like. If you clear the browser’s data, they disappear.
  • The ratings are calculated on your phone. The best rating for each scene is saved in the browser’s storage on your phone, as numbers, so that you can see your result for a level. If you have said yes to the face analysis, two numbers about your face are included: how much it acted and how often your eyes were down in the script. The ratings are not sent anywhere, and you remove them with the Clear my results button.
  • Your answer to the question about the face analysis is saved on your phone, so that you do not have to answer again. You can change it on the recording screen.
  • The browser’s storage on your phone keeps count of your 5 free minutes with the AI scene partner. If you have Plus, the licence code that shows it is saved too. None of this is cookies, and none of it says anything about who you are.

If you buy Plus

  • The purchase is made on Stripe’s payment page. Stripe receives your email and card details and processes them under its own privacy policy. We never see your card number.
  • At Stripe we see your email, what you have paid and when, and whether you have cancelled or withdrawn from Plus.
  • The licence code on your phone contains the number of your subscription and your customer number at Stripe, but not your email.
  • When you buy Plus you get a restore code. It is saved on your phone so that you can see it again. Stripe only keeps a check value of the code, not the code itself.
  • To count the AI minutes, we save at Netlify how many seconds each subscription has used per month, and how many free seconds have been used per day by everyone together. Nothing about what was said in the conversations is saved there.
  • If you withdraw from the purchase, the name you write and the time are saved at Stripe, as proof that you have withdrawn.
  • The details of the purchase are kept for as long as the Swedish Bookkeeping Act requires.

If you sign up or answer the questions

  • The signup saves your email, where you heard about us, whether you want to test the Android app, which language the page was in and that you said yes. We only use it to email you about ActWith AI.
  • The questions save your answers, and your email if you leave it.
  • The data is saved in Netlify Forms at Netlify, Inc. in the USA. Netlify also saves your IP address with each submission, and this cannot be turned off.
  • The signup is kept until you ask us to delete it, and at most 12 months without us emailing. The answers to the questions are kept for at most 12 months.
  • Email us if you want to see or delete your data.

The person responsible for the site and contact details will be added before the site opens to everyone.
